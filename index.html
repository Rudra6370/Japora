How It Works

  1. 1

    Start a Session

    Open Japora and tap "Start Japa" on the home screen. The timer begins and your count resets.

  2. 2

    Count Your Mantras

    Tap the progress ring or use volume buttons (+1 up, -1 down) to count each mantra. Voice counting also available.

  3. 3

    Save & Track

    When you finish, the session is automatically saved to history. View your progress, streaks, and weekly summaries.

  4. 4

    Review & Improve

    Check your heatmap, calendar view, and spiritual insights to understand your practice patterns and improve.
