Support & FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I reset my progress?

Go to Settings > Data > Clear all jaap records. Confirm the action to reset all your sessions, streaks, and counts. This cannot be undone.

How do I enable or disable voice counting?

Go to Settings > Voice Counting and toggle the option on or off. When enabled, tap the mic button on the Home screen to count by speaking.

How do I change the app theme?

Go to Settings > Themes and select your preferred theme: Default, Temple Gold, Shiva Blue, Forest Meditation, or Dark AMOLED.

How do I backup my data?

Go to Settings > Backup & Restore > Create Backup. Your data will be exported as a CSV file that you can share or save.

How do I restore my data from a backup?

Go to Settings > Backup & Restore > Restore Backup and select the CSV file you previously exported. All your sessions, streaks, and settings will be restored.

How do I enable or disable the home screen widget?

Go to Settings > Widget to toggle the widget on or off. The widget shows your current count, today's count, and progress toward your target.

Why are ads not showing or why is the app ad-free?

Japora may show ads via Yandex Mobile Ads. If you've enabled ad-free mode from Settings, all banners and interstitials are hidden. If no ad unit IDs are configured, ads are disabled for release builds. Debug builds use Yandex demo units.

Troubleshooting

App won't open

Try restarting the app. If the issue persists, uninstall and reinstall the app. Your data will be preserved if you have a backup.

Widget not updating

Ensure the widget is enabled in Settings. If the widget still doesn't update, go to Android Settings > Apps > Japora > Permissions and ensure all required permissions are granted.

Voice counting not working

Ensure the microphone permission is granted. Go to Android Settings > Apps > Japora > Permissions > Microphone. Also ensure you're tapping the mic button on the Home screen.

Sessions not saving

Ensure you're tapping "End & Save" when finishing a session. If the issue persists, check that you have sufficient storage on your device, as Hive stores data locally.

Permission-Related Help

Japora requires certain permissions to function properly. Here's what each permission does and how to manage them:

  • Phone/Network State: Required for Yandex Mobile Ads to determine when to show ads. Without this, ads are automatically disabled.
  • Vibrate: Required for haptic feedback on each count. Can be disabled in Settings if you don't want vibration.
  • Record Audio: Required for the voice counting feature. The app accesses the microphone only when you tap the mic button on the Home screen.
  • Notifications: Required for Android 13+ to display session completion notifications. Can be managed in Android Settings.

To manage permissions: Go to Android Settings > Apps > Japora > Permissions and toggle the permissions on or off.

Installation and Usage Instructions

  1. Install Japora from the Google Play Store or by sideloading the APK.
  2. On first launch, complete the onboarding flow (4 pages).
  3. Grant the required permissions when prompted (Internet, Storage, Microphone for voice counting).
  4. Start your first jaap session by tapping "Start Japa" on the home screen.
  5. Customize your experience via Settings > Appearance, Settings > Data, and Settings > Widget.
  6. To enable the home screen widget, long-press your home screen > Widgets > Drag Japora widget to the desired location.
